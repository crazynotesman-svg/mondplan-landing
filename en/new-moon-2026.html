What does the new moon mean?

Sleep

Some people report deeper sleep during new moon nights - this is less scientifically studied than the full moon effect.

Gardening

The new moon is a rest phase - favorable for soil work, weeding and sowing planning.

Rituals

In many cultures, the new moon is the classic time for new beginnings, setting intentions and starting new projects.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the next new moon in 2026?

The first new moon of 2026 is on January 18 at 20:54 CET. Eleven more follow - one per month.

Why is the new moon invisible?

During a new moon, the moon stands between Earth and Sun. The lit hemisphere faces away from Earth, so we cannot see the moon in the night sky.

Is the new moon good for gardening?

In biodynamic farming, the new moon is a rest phase - favorable for soil work, weeding and planning the next sowing.

How accurate are these times?

Times are calculated with the standard algorithm from Jean Meeus' 'Astronomical Algorithms' (chapter 49). Accuracy plus/minus 1-2 minutes.
