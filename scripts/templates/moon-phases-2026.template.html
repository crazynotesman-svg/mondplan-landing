The eight moon phases

The lunar cycle - a synodic month of 29.53 days - runs through eight primary phases.

  1. New Moon

    The moon is between Earth and the Sun; the lit side faces away from us. Traditional time for rest and planning.

  2. Waxing Crescent

    A thin sliver grows on the right. Growth energy is traditionally considered to be rising.

  3. First Quarter

    Half of the disc is lit. A traditional turning point in the cycle toward growth.

  4. Waxing Gibbous

    More than half lit, still growing. Good time for planting and transplanting in traditional practice.

  5. Full Moon

    The whole disc is lit. Traditionally favored for harvesting and gathering.

  6. Waning Gibbous

    Light begins to recede from the right. Traditionally a settling, pruning time.

  7. Last Quarter

    Half lit again, waning side. Traditional end-of-growth turning point.

  8. Waning Crescent

    A thin sliver remains on the left. Time for pruning, weeding and soil work.
