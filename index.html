Questions, answered

A biodynamic moon calendar connects the lunar cycle - a synodic month of about 29.53 days - with everyday activities such as gardening, haircare, and rest. Traditional biodynamic practice divides the cycle into waxing and waning halves: waxing periods are associated with growth and vitality, while waning periods are associated with settling, pruning, and rest. MondPlan turns this practice into a simple iOS app.

In biodynamic tradition, the waxing moon (from New Moon to Full Moon) is a good time to sow seeds, plant, and transplant, because growth energy is believed to rise. The Full Moon favors harvesting, while the waning moon (from Full Moon to New Moon) is often used for pruning, weeding, and soil work. MondPlan marks these windows on a calendar so you can plan garden tasks around them.
