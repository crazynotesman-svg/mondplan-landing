Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

The short version

MondPlan does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no cloud sync. Everything you enter in the app - moon phase bookmarks, sleep notes, and mood entries - is stored locally on your device and never leaves it.

Information we do not collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Health or biometric data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Purchase history beyond what Apple provides through the App Store

Local data storage

All app data (mood entries, sleep notes, calendar preferences) is stored in the app's local storage on your device. Removing the app deletes this data permanently. No backup is made to our servers because no data is ever sent to our servers.

Apple App Store

MondPlan is distributed through the Apple App Store. When you purchase or download the app, Apple's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to that transaction. We do not receive your personal information from Apple beyond standard, aggregate sales figures.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children. There is nothing to delete, share, or sell.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Because we collect no data, changes will typically relate to wording or App Store requirements rather than data practices.
